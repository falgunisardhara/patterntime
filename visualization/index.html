PatternTime User Guide & Methodology

PatternTime is a locally running AI/ML time-series investigation laboratory designed to ingest application logs and metrics, detect structural change points, and measure statistical impact.


Algorithms Available:

  • Sliding Window: Compares means between two consecutive sliding windows. Best for abrupt step changes.
  • CUSUM (Cumulative Sum): Tracks cumulative standardized deviations above baseline. Best for detecting persistent drift.
  • Robust CUSUM: Incorporates Winsorization outlier clipping (clips observations with $|z| > \text{clip\_z}$). Immune to transient single-request spikes.
  • EWMA (Exponentially Weighted Moving Average): Applies recursive exponential smoothing with time-varying control limits. Ideal for early detection of sustained shifts.
  • Compare All: Evaluates all 4 algorithms concurrently to provide a consensus scorecard.

Statistical Effect Size (Cohen's d):

Calculated as $d = (\mu_{\text{post}} - \mu_{\text{pre}}) / \sigma_{\text{pooled}}$. Categorized as Negligible ($< 0.2$), Low ($0.2-0.5$), Moderate ($0.5-1.0$), High ($1.0-2.5$), or Critical ($> 2.5$).
