Understanding the Numbers
🛡

What does "99.9% Certainty" mean?

The system uses Welch's t-test and Cohen's effect size to separate real slowdowns from random noise. 99.9% certainty means this is not a one-off slow user — the server pipeline genuinely degraded across thousands of requests.

⏳

Why is "Worst Case (p95)" more important than average?

Averages hide pain. The p95 represents the slowest 5% of your real customers. If p95 is 2.5 seconds, hundreds of users experienced visible delays or timeouts — even if the average looks acceptable.
